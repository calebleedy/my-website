% ================= INTERESTS =================
\section*{Interests}
The goal of my career is to help people make better decisions under uncertainty.
I believe that statistics offers a useful framework for incorporating additional
information to make more scientific decisions while understanding the limits of
our knowledge. In graduate school, I created methods to combine data sources from
probability and non-probability surveys. I am interested in overcoming real-world
difficulties with data and I am excited to partner with businesses to help clients
find solutions to real data problems.
